## 03 / SELECTED WORK

*A selection of projects that represent my approach to building software.*

### 01 — PRINTFLOW

**Digital Order Management System for Print Shops**

A platform designed to bring traditional print-shop ordering online.

Customers can upload documents, configure printing requirements, calculate prices and submit payment proof. Shop owners can manage incoming orders and verify payments through a centralized workflow.

**Engineering highlights**
- Multi-document upload and individual print configurations.
- Dynamic pricing based on printing specifications.
- Order lifecycle and payment verification workflows.
- Authentication and cloud-based file management.

`Java 21` `Spring Boot` `PostgreSQL` `React` `TypeScript` `JWT`

[EXPLORE REPOSITORY →](https://github.com/Sjking2025/printflow-order-management)

---

### 02 — CAMPUS PLACEMENT PLATFORM

**A Centralized Campus Recruitment Ecosystem**

A placement management platform concept designed to simplify campus recruitment operations and eliminate fragmented placement workflows.

Designed around four distinct roles: Students, Department Officers, Placement Coordinators and Administrators.

**Engineering focus**
- Role-based access and management workflows.
- Student eligibility and shortlisting.
- Recruitment drive and application management.
- Centralized placement operations and analytics.

`MERN Stack` `Role-Based Architecture` `REST APIs`
